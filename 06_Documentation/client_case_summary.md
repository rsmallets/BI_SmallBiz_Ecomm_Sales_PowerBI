# Small Business E-Commerce BI Case Study

## Business Problem
The client relied on raw CSV exports to track sales and profitability, leading to inconsistent reporting and manual errors.

## Solution
- Built SQL staging table from raw export
- Cleaned and standardized data
- Designed star schema (Fact + Dimensions)
- Created executive Power BI dashboard

## Impact
Enabled leadership to monitor revenue, profit, margin, and category performance in under 30 seconds.
